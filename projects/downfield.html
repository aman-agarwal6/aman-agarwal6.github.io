Downfield

A phone-first NFL research app that turns public football context into structured, versioned reports, with explicit source gaps and uncertainty.

The problem

A fluent football report can still rely on stale injuries, unsupported player roles or internally inconsistent forecasts. The hard part is making those gaps visible before a user relies on the output.

Downfield assembles recent games, roster/injury information, news and weather into a research packet. The model proposes forecasts and their football case. The application retains the inputs, checks the report and keeps later revisions separate from the original.

Stage 01 / Evidence packet

What is known before the model starts?

Public game, player, injury, news and weather observations are assembled with source snapshots and named gaps. Original input and optional price observations are retained.

Boundary

A cached screen cannot establish fresh injury information or current price availability.

Stage 02 / Research worker

What authority does the model receive?

A subscription-authenticated local CLI runs with fixed settings, bounded inputs, restricted tools and an environment allowlist. Fresh work depends on the computer being awake and online.

Boundary

Webpages are untrusted evidence, not instructions. A permitted fetch does not prove the page supports the claim.

Stage 03 / Deterministic checks

Is the report internally consistent?

The contract checks shape, identities, retained baselines, arithmetic, source provenance and broad cross-player totals. Missing estimates and unverified citations remain visible.

Boundary

Schema validity and consistent arithmetic do not establish correct football assumptions or a forecasting edge.

Stage 04 / Version & publish

What changes when the forecast changes?

Each report preserves its evidence and price snapshots. A separate, expiring report-only capability sends football reports to BetTail; grading can leave missing statistics ungraded.

Boundary

This connection does not reuse app login/database credentials. Full-slate review and live restart behavior still need verification.

Decisions & tradeoffs

A citation isn't its own proof

Source provenance is assigned by the application from observed retrieval, not by the model. Uncaptured model citations stay unverified. An observed fetch establishes access and timing; a person still needs to check what the source actually supports.

Validate beyond JSON shape

The validators reconcile forecasts with retained baselines and check cross-player totals, identity and coverage. This catches contradictions while leaving the football assumptions open to review. It cannot turn an uncalibrated estimate into a proven edge.

Run locally with constrained authority

The worker uses a subscription-authenticated CLI, restricted tools and an environment allowlist. That keeps research separate from application credentials, but the computer must be awake, online and running Downfield for fresh reports.

Keep publishing narrow

The BetTail connection sends football reports through a separate, expiring publication capability. It does not reuse user login or database credentials. This adds a distinct connection to maintain, but avoids giving the research worker general database access.

What can be inspected

Inspect forecast selection, revision handling and focused test results ↗

The local source separates the research worker, report contract, provenance checks, publication limits and forecast evaluation. Tests use synthetic data; actual subscription runs and live public-source checks are recorded separately in the project documentation.

  • Original game, evidence and optional price snapshots stay with a report.
  • Report versions preserve earlier predictions when new information changes the forecast.
  • Missing inputs are represented as gaps or explicit exclusions instead of invented detail.
  • Postgame grading can leave missing statistics ungraded.

This case study was prepared from local source and documentation in September 2026. No live model run, scheduling trial or forecasting benchmark was performed for this portfolio pass.

What remains open

  • Initial full-slate population and content review remain in progress.
  • Live operation after restart still needs verification; a configured schedule alone is not evidence of a completed run.
  • Model probabilities are uncalibrated judgments. Valid reports and green tests do not establish profitable predictions.
  • Public-source availability can change. The app cannot establish current price availability without a real observation.

AI use: AI coding agents wrote substantial portions of the implementation under my direction. The research worker itself uses an LLM; its prose, probability estimates and source interpretation require review. Downfield does not place wagers.
